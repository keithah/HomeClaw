Refreshed App Store screenshots showing the new automation capabilities introduced in HomeClaw:

• Add conditions to existing automations in place — tighten any rule without rebuilding it. Button bindings and Siri references survive.
• Time-of-day triggers — fire at a clock time or relative to sunrise/sunset.
• Auto-revert — trigger actions, then revert them after N seconds. Great for motion-triggered porch lights.

The macOS menu bar app, CLI, MCP server, and OpenClaw plugin all expose these new verbs. Everything stays local.
